<h3>
UW Practicum: Interpretive Signage
</h3>
<p>
These are files from my practicum project as part of the UW Wetland Science and Management Certificate. The assignment required students to characterize a wetland according to typical methods in a critical areas study, then study the wetland through another project. My project was conceptual interpretive signage. Years later, this project still floats around my head. I particularly like my ideas for a "How to look at a plant" sign.
</p>
